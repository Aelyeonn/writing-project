Summary & Conclusion

This document explains how to carry out Machine Translation Post-Editing (MTPE) efficiently. In MTPE, a professional translator reviews and corrects raw machine translation output so that it becomes accurate, smooth, and consistent in less time than translating from scratch. The document distinguishes Light MTPE (fixing only errors in meaning, grammar, and spelling) from Full MTPE (also improving flow, terminology, and style). Preparation involves a CAT tool such as Smartcat or Trados, an MT engine such as DeepL or Google Translate, a glossary, a style guide, and bilingual dictionaries. The workflow has three phases: pre-assessment and setup, core post-editing, and quality assurance and proofreading. Sample Indonesian–English comparisons show the editor replacing literal phrasing with natural wording, fixing grammar, and leaving correct output unchanged.
